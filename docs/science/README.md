# Heurēka!

Heurēka! is the second app in this repo, after Q.E.D.!. It covers "the everything else of STEM, plus business": physics, chemistry, biology and medical calculations, earth and space science, engineering, computer science, and business (personal finance, accounting, economics, startups and venture capital). The name is the original Greek spelling of Archimedes' "I found it!".

It reuses Q.E.D.!'s engine, animation kit, animals and art library, and mixes microgame styles: Q.E.D.!-style working (rewrite one problem step by step), Holy Moly!-style scenes (one shouted action that acts out a concept), and new mechanics (predict, build, tune, sandbox, rhythm, debug, role-play). Topics are chosen and ordered by how much they matter for real jobs and projects, with standard course outlines as sources.

The curriculum is being drafted in this folder, one file per subject:
- CURRICULUM_physics.md
- CURRICULUM_chem_bio.md (chemistry, biology, medical calculations)
- CURRICULUM_earth_eng.md (earth and space science, engineering)
- CURRICULUM_cs.md (computer science, including a coding-interview patterns unit that could grow into its own app)
- CURRICULUM_business.md

The app itself has not been built yet; the folder layout for its code will be decided when building starts.
