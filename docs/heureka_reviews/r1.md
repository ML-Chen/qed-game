### motion_goal GOAL! — FIX (roughly/roughly/roughly) — "GOAL!" says kick it in; the game says stop it gently inside an open-backed goal, which is not what goals do
- The rule "ball must come to REST inside the goal; too hard rolls out the back" fights the word GOAL and every kid's soccer intuition (a real net stops the ball). Swap the goal for a target that is about stopping: a painted circle/"parking bay" on the grass, or a hole/cup (mini-golf) the ball has to stop in. Change cmd to e.g. "STOP IN THE RING!" or keep GOAL only if the net actually catches anything that enters.
- Friction (the win text) is invisible: as the ball rolls, draw grass blades bending/dust puffs and a shrinking speed trail so "the grass slows it" reads without the card. Also the play strip is a thin band at y~470 with 2/3 sky: zoom the scene so the ball and goal are bigger.

### forces_lift LIFT! — FIX (roughly/no/roughly) — the rock is launched high into the sky like a catapult, which is not what a lever lift looks like
